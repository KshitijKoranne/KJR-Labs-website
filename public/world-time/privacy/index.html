World Time for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026 · Version 1.0

World Time collects no data about you. There is nothing to opt out of, nothing to delete from a server, and no account to close.

What the app collects

Nothing. World Time has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party software development kits.

The app makes no network requests of any kind. It cannot send data anywhere, because it never connects to anything.

What is stored, and where

Three things are held in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • the list of cities you track
  • which city you have set as home
  • your two settings — clock format and night overlay

This data never leaves the device. It is removed completely when you delete the app. It is included in an encrypted iPhone backup only if you have backups switched on, and that backup belongs to you, not to us.

Where the times come from

Time zones and daylight saving rules come from the time zone database built into iOS. Sunrise, sunset and day length are calculated on the device from each city's latitude and the current date. No clock server, weather service or location service is contacted, and the app does not request access to your location.

Permissions

World Time asks for no permissions. It does not use location, contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone, notifications or the network.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Purchases

World Time has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes. The date at the top always shows when this page was last changed.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct and erase personal data held about you. No personal data is held, so there is nothing to access, correct or erase. You may still write with any question and it will be answered.
